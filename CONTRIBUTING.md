Read [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) before changing the repository. It defines the
baseline check, frozen contracts, change discipline, validation gate, and
commit format.

intentional contribution submitted for inclusion in SEE-ME SEE-U is provided
under the Apache License 2.0, without additional terms or conditions, as set
out in Section 5 of [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

the right to contribute. Third-party code, geometry, fonts, sound, demo-derived
data, and generated assets must retain their original licence, provenance, and
attribution beside the material and in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) where applicable.

The source-code licence does not grant rights to the artwork's name, brand, or
